# Business Requirements & Community Model

## 1. Zero-Cost Campus Community Service

**SEU Adda** operates as a free, open-source, non-profit community platform for Southeast University students and alumni:
- **No Monetization Paywalls**: All features (rooms, music, confessions, dialogues, photo cards) are 100% free and open to everyone without restrictions.
- **Zero Advertising**: No third-party ad networks, popups, banner ads, or tracking cookies that detract from the campus atmosphere.

# Glossary of Campus Terminology & Technical Terms

| Term | Category | Bengali Meaning & Context |
|---|---|---|
| **Adda (আড্ডা)** | Culture | Informal, spontaneous, lighthearted gathering of university friends filled with stories, tea, laughter, and camaraderie. |
| **Lal Cha (লাল চা)** | Campus Life | Hot spiced red tea without milk, the staple beverage of university students across Bangladesh. |
| **Chyakka (ছ্যাঁকা)** | Campus Slang | Romantic heartbreak or disappointment, a common comedic theme among university peer groups. |
| **CR (Class Representative)** | University | The student leader who coordinates class schedules, assignments, and announcements between teachers and students. |
| **Mama (মামা)** | Campus Culture | Affectionate, respectful title for tea stall vendors, gatekeepers, and campus cafeteria staff. |
| **Canteen (ক্যান্টিন)** | Campus Life | The university cafeteria where students grab quick snacks, parathas, and tea between lectures. |
| **Batch (ব্যাচ)** | University | The cohort year or intake group of students enrolled in a department (e.g., *52nd Batch*). |
| **Headless Audio** | Technical | Playing audio via an invisible or offscreen YouTube IFrame without rendering a video player interface. |
| **Ephemeral Room** | Technical | A temporary virtual room with a strictly enforced 2-hour lifespan that self-destructs after expiration. |
| **Speech Bubble** | Technical | A CSS-styled floating dialogue tooltip that appears above a character avatar's coordinates for 30 seconds. |
| **Hotspot** | Technical | A calibrated percentage-based coordinate area on the campus illustration representing a clickable character. |
| **Auto-Healing** | Technical | Backend resilience logic that automatically recreates a missing database room record when a valid message arrives. |
| **POSIX Slash** | Technical | Standard forward slash (`/`) path separators used by Unix/Linux file systems and web archives. |
